                                                                   VA HOSPITAL/MEDICAL SCHOOL PINK SHUTTLE
 The route runs from approximately 6:00 AM until 6:30 PM Monday through Friday with no service on the holidays and recess periods. This service provides
 two shuttles running at the same daily times but departing from different locations. Both routes and the order of the stops are referenced above. The duration
 time of the shuttle loop varies and is dependent upon the number of passengers, and weather/ traffic conditions. All shuttles when completing the loop,
 return to the start point and begin again. All shuttles can be tracked on the Yale Shuttle Tracking URL on the Yale website.
