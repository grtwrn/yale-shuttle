## Observable frontend sensitivity, without outcome claims

Separate, explicitly synthetic mechanism fixtures exercised ranking and
reminder branches. With a Red competitor, the directed K8 fixture changed the
initial order from Red/Brown/Walk to Brown/Red/Walk and immediately issued
`leave_now`; the other fixtures kept Red first. With Brown alone, the original
fixtures issued `heads_up`, directed K8 issued `leave_now` immediately, and
directed K5 issued `heads_up` followed by `leave_now` 21 seconds later. These
states all matched independently mounted original components.

Those deliberately synthetic bounds have a separate model identity. They show
that the integrated component can change recommendations and reminders, not
that one fitted candidate improves them. There are no boarding, accuracy,
waiting-cost or missed-shuttle outcome results from this integration, and
neither K has been selected.
