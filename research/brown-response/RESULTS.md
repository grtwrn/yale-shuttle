## Limits retained for the next integration

The all-target wire tests are synthetic; development log rows were explicitly
wrapped as synthetic responses. They are not claimed to be historical complete
public captures. The next pipeline must join full raw/provider prefixes to
actual captured `server_eta.at`, retain exact response bytes and receipt order,
load only actually sealed model artifacts, and invoke the separate reviewed
whole-app scenario adapter. Rolling artifacts are a separate root-owned task.

Brown geometry currently requires exact frozen route order, stop coordinates
and path. A different derived public path is an explicit incompatibility; this
gate does not invent an occurrence remapping or imply service/model failure.
Missing causal inputs and unavailable artifacts preserve the exact served row.
Scientific physical/source/path/fit discrepancies still halt the comparison.

`ArrivalDetails.tsx` continues to display the unchanged distribution dots when
available. Its plot and probability consumers therefore remain tied to the old
distribution even when the low/high envelope changes. This is a diagnostic
hybrid, not a coherent probability export or a production-ready forecast.
Prospective labels, scoring and any deployment remain unopened and unlaunched.
