## Established boundaries

- `freeze_prefix` copies a caller-selected sequence-zero prefix through a completed record into a new directory and seals its exact journal/manifest bytes and referenced bodies. The source remains untouched. An interrupted copy without a seal is unusable. An independently retained seal hash is required; a hash chain alone cannot authenticate a wholesale rewrite.
- `iter_verified` validates the complete sealed journal hash before yielding, then checks every sequence/link, metadata field set, public endpoint, transport flag, request/receipt ordering, original raw/gzip hash and size, and bounded complete single-member decompression. It retains recorder records and raw bodies. Missing, reordered, altered, truncated or inconsistent inputs fail closed. Callers must consume the final `verification-complete` event before publishing output; a late failure invalidates the whole result.
- Complete empty fleets and missing/stale ETA remain actual frontend inputs. Parseable HTTP/partial failures do not become successful empty fleets. Duplicate-key bodies and unsafe clocks remain unknown and nonadmissible. The existing envelope explicitly refuses these unknown inputs until a future runner's uncertainty handling is separately reviewed.
- Schedule gaps remain distinct from fleet responses. Manifest snapshots may precede or follow the fixed prefix. Terminal manifest skipped-tick excess caused by the recorder's increment-before-append ordering is reported without inventing its position; unpersisted response metadata remains explicit.
- Complete HTML/resource observations never combine old or later modules. Different builds, failed/incomplete resources, unknown/conflicting proofs and unsafe clocks remain explicit. A proof discovered later cannot label an earlier receipt. Equal health brackets still allow an unobserved intermediate deployment/rollback.
- Every request/gap emits a release state. Its exact sequence and ID link each fleet receipt to the knowledge available then. Unknown states have no top-level source/tree/files and shadow older supported mappings. The envelope integration fixture proves that retaining a newer unknown prevents selecting an older supported version.
- A later valid same-build health response can resolve an isolated health failure using an earlier complete bundle under the explicit continuity assumption. Its candidate knowledge time includes the later successful receipt; the failed interval stays unknown and the cumulative failure count remains. A different observed build still requires a new complete bundle observation.
- Causal release states and bracket diagnostics carry null knowledge time after unsafe wall clocks while preserving their observed timestamp and journal position. No sorting, timestamp repair, retrospective bracket assignment or future-proof backdating occurs.
