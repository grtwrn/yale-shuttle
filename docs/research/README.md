# Shuttle ETA and rider-experience research

This is the public research archive for the shuttle app, including unsuccessful experiments, independent reviews, measurement corrections, analysis source, and numerical results. The snapshot was assembled on **September 18, 2026**. Historical reports retain their original dates and conclusions; a report saying “ready for review” does not establish that its proposal shipped.

Start with the findings below, then the [complete report catalogue](catalogue.md). The [file manifest](manifest.json) records the original artifact paths, sizes, available hashes, publication transformations, and omitted inputs. [Reproduction and data scope](REPRODUCING.md) explains what can be checked from the repository and what requires the original recording archive.

## Reading the archive

- **Implemented** behavior is documented in the repository's current design documents and merged code. Research snapshots are evidence, not alternate running implementations.
- **Experimental** includes promising component results whose inputs, subgroup support or full rider impact remain insufficient for production.
- **Rejected or superseded** experiments remain available, including first results before causal/identity corrections. Read the later review before relying on their scores.
- **Descriptive** counts, correlations and weighted history summaries do not establish a driver policy or calibrated future probability.

The principal rider case is Red waiting at **344 Winchester**, then reaching **Division / Prospect** for pickup, with **Rosenkranz** as a destination. A pickup window and a destination window are different outcomes. Reports also cover other routes and repeated stop occurrences; their conclusions should not be transferred without checking scope.

The archived source preserves original execution paths and historical assumptions. Compact JSON results explicitly identify any removed per-observation arrays; they must not be substituted for an original hash-verified input. Operational rider submissions, browser interaction captures, execution logs, bulk databases and raw GPS/forecast streams are outside this Git snapshot. Their absence is recorded, not treated as missing trips or model successes.

The latest bunching screen tested prespecified regimes and predicted ETA horizons, including eight later holds with frozen coefficients. Older close-leader gains reversed on September 18, and only one fresh hold activated the supported gate. No subset was promoted. The Pi load constraint stopped further work before individual raw-regression audits; the reports identify that unfinished work.

Further bunching work should evaluate supported, prespecified situations and predicted ETA horizons separately, with fallback elsewhere. A useful subset is a valid improvement; selecting a subset after seeing its favorable outcomes is not validation.
